Privacy policy · November 26, 2026
Developer: Oana Rinaldi

No account, no tracking

Dress by the Weather has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Preferences, comfort feedback, saved looks, cities and packing lists are stored on your device. iOS device backups may include this local data.

Optional location

If you allow location access, the app uses your approximate location while it is open to request a forecast. You can instead select any city or address. Change permission in iOS Settings at any time. Coordinates are truncated to four decimal places before forecast requests.

Weather requests and third parties

Forecast requests go directly to MET Norway. MET Norway receives the requested coordinates and your IP address. Its API access logs may retain these values in its data centre in Oslo, Norway. This applies to searched destinations as well as current location. The developer does not operate a server that receives or stores these requests. Forecast responses are cached locally to reduce network traffic.

Place searches

City and address searches and place-name lookup use Apple MapKit. Search queries are sent to Apple under Apple's privacy terms. Location permission is not required for manually searching places.

Reminders and widgets

Optional reminders are scheduled locally after you grant notification permission. Widgets share a small local snapshot of the last forecast opened in the app, including the city and outfit tip. They do not request your location or poll the weather in the background.

Deletion

Delete saved looks and trips in the app. Removing the app removes its local data; copies may remain in iOS backups. You can manage those backups in iOS Settings. The developer has no account database to delete.
