# Editorial wardrobe library

The app includes **214 reviewed Midjourney couple photographs** and a generated glass app icon. Generation used the owner's signed-in Midjourney browser and existing GPU allowance; no extra credits were purchased.

## Temperature coverage

| Band | Estimated feels-like temperature | Typical layers |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Below −15°C | Expedition parka, thermals, insulated trousers and boots |
| 1 | −15 to −5°C | Insulated coat, knitwear, gloves, winter boots |
| 2 | −5 to 5°C | Warm coat, wool layers, full trousers, boots |
| 3 | 5 to 10°C | Coat, warm knit, closed footwear |
| 4 | 10 to 15°C | Trench or jacket, knit, trousers |
| 5 | 15 to 20°C | Light jacket or cardigan, breathable layers |
| 6 | 20 to 25°C | Light shirt, breathable trousers or shorts |
| 7 | 25 to 30°C | Airy linen, light trousers or shorts, sun protection |
| 8 | 30 to 35°C | Loose, breathable clothing, shade and water |
| 9 | 35°C and above | Very light breathable layers; avoid strenuous heat exposure |

Each band has Everyday, Office, Evening and Active collections. Cold active looks are walking inspiration, not specialist mountaineering equipment.

## Weather coverage

- Clear, partly cloudy, overcast and fog use the temperature-matched dry collections.
- Rain and showers have separate coat / umbrella collections grouped at 5–10, 10–15, 20–25 and 30–35°C. Colder rain uses winter protection.
- Snow and sleet have winter collections grouped at −15 to −5 and −5 to 5°C.
- Wind changes the clothing checklist and adds wind protection; photos remain editorial illustrations.
- Thunderstorms, extreme heat and extreme cold display cautions. The app does not claim to replace official warnings or specialist outdoor safety advice.

## Review and source records

`catalogue.json` lists every collection, generation job ID, prompt and approved variant index. `jobs.json` records 280 generated fashion candidates. The shipped assets are optimized JPEG encodings of reviewed originals. Rejected images include unsuitable cold/rain footwear, additional people or poor framing. Four rain collections use approved alternatives for the same weather and temperature.

Full-resolution originals can be downloaded again by job ID into ignored `Art/Originals/`; temporary copies were cleared after import to recover disk space. Run `Scripts/import_art.py` after downloading the approved originals. The app's asset catalog contains the actual production images.

Photographs depict fictional AI-generated models. Their backgrounds do not represent live conditions at the selected destination. Clothing checklists are computed from the forecast separately.
