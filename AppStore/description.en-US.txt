Your forecast. Your outfit. One less decision.

Dress by the Weather turns worldwide forecasts into thoughtful outfit suggestions for women and men, with original fashion photography and a calm, glossy native iPhone and iPad experience.

FREE EVERY DAY
• Check the weather where you are, or search cities and addresses around the world. Current location is optional.
• Explore hourly forecasts and the available daily outlook, with destination-local times.
• Find layers for the temperature, rain, snow and wind, personalized to how warm or cold you feel.
• Choose Everyday, Office, Evening or Active looks, see clothing checklists, and save or share favorites.
• Build basic destination packing lists, set a local morning reminder, and use Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets.

MAKE IT PERSONAL WITH PRO
An optional one-time purchase permanently unlocks your personal style studio. No subscription.
• Dress the week: save occasions and outfit variations for the next seven available forecast days.

No app account. No ads. Wardrobe photos and personal plans stay on your device.

Global weather is supplied by MET Norway, with attribution in the app. Forecast availability varies; feels-like temperatures are estimates. Sample mode is clearly labeled and never presented as live weather. Clothing advice is general lifestyle guidance, not protective equipment or a guarantee of comfort.
